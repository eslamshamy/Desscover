News from Dessau

    About the city

    Dessau-Roßlau, a twin city on the Elbe and the Mulde, holds four UNESCO World Heritage listings, more than almost any other town in Germany.

    The Bauhaus. From 1925 to 1932 the Bauhaus school made Dessau a centre of modernism. Architects such as Walter Gropius and Ludwig Mies van der Rohe and artists such as Wassily Kandinsky and László Moholy-Nagy worked here.

    The Garden Realm Dessau-Wörlitz. Palaces, parks, gardens and lakes set in a cultivated landscape of about 142 km², a World Heritage site since 2000.

    The Middle Elbe Biosphere Reserve. Some 126,000 hectares around the city, one of the last near-natural river landscapes in Europe.

    Luther’s lectures on Romans. A student’s notes of Martin Luther’s lectures of 1515–16, Documentary Heritage since 2016, are kept in the Anhalt State Library in Dessau.

    Lonely Planet named Dessau one of its best places to visit in 2027. The tourist information offices are at Ratsgasse 11 in Dessau and Hauptstraße 11 in Roßlau.

    Source and travel tips: visitdessau.com

      About

      Digital Dessau beta is developed by Eslam ElShamy, an architect and designer based in Dessau.

      It brings the city's landmarks together on one map, as 3D models you can explore across time, walk through and redesign, with stories drawn from the sources cited on every card. The models are reconstructions from published dimensions, photographs and descriptions, so some details are approximate.

      Built with three.js. Base map: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Models drawn for this project.
